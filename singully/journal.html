Behind the build.

14 Feb 2026 · 3 min read

First stitch.

The first piece, before it had a name.

The first thing made for Edition 01 was not the piece that photographs well. It was a swatch, worked over a weekend, to see whether the stitch could carry the weight of a full garment.

It could. Everything since has been about not getting in its way.

22 Mar 2026 · 2 min read

Testing ₹3,500.

A number that had to hold up in the studio, not just on a slide.

Three to five thousand rupees is easy to write down and harder to build a piece against. We priced a shirt at ₹3,500 and worked backward, checking what the maker's share and the cotton and the hours actually cost.

The number held. That is the only reason it stayed.

30 Apr 2026 · 3 min read

The tag is the product.

Not packaging around the piece. Part of it.

Early samples put the maker's name on a care label, folded into the seam where nobody looks. That version got thrown out. The tag now sits where a size tag usually sits, and it says more than a size ever did.

Credit as the product. Dignity is the operating model, not the marketing.

18 Jun 2026 · 4 min read

Finding the hand.

The search is the content. Not a finished collaboration announced after the fact.

We are not looking for a name to attach to a press release. We are looking for someone very good at one thing, who has not yet been asked to put their name on their own work.

Edition 02 is still in that search. When the hand is found, this page changes before anything else does.

09 Jul 2026 · 2 min read

Why we will never discount.

A discount on a piece is a pay cut for the person who made it.

A share of what a piece sells for only means something if the price stays where it was set. Cut the price at the end of a season and the maker's share is cut with it, quietly, after the fact.

So the price on day one is the price on the last day. That is the whole rule.

21 Aug 2026 · 3 min read

A carpet becomes a coat.

A carpet that had a floor becomes a coat that has a person.

The Carpet Coat started as exactly that, a carpet, worn at the edges, headed for a skip. Cutting into it felt wrong for about an hour, then it stopped feeling wrong and started feeling obvious.

We do not manufacture. We transform. This is what that sentence looks like on a table.
