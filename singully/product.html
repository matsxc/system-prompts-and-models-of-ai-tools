The piece

A plain cotton shirt is the canvas. Heavy hand embroidery, worked over three days, is the rest.

No two pieces in this run carry the same density of stitch. Roughly thirty percent of a piece is the canvas. Seventy percent is the soul.

The hands

This piece is one of ten made by a single hand for Edition 01. A salary first. Then a share of what they made.

The maker is not our labour. The maker is our co-creator.

The tag

Every piece carries a woven tag: the maker's mark, the piece number, and Sinगली. No price. No size. Just credit.

The tag is not packaging. It is part of the piece.

Care

Hand wash in cold water. Dry flat, out of direct sun. Iron on the reverse, over a cloth, away from the stitch.

Shipping and returns
