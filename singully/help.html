Help.

Sizing, shipping, care, privacy and terms.

01 / Sizing

Cut in XS to XL.

Every piece is cut across sizes XS to XL. Because a run is small and cut in small numbers, the fit can vary slightly from one edition to the next, so the size on the label is a starting point, not the whole answer.

Each product page carries the actual measurements in centimetres: chest, length, shoulder, whatever the piece calls for. Measure something you already own and compare it against those numbers before you order.

02 / Shipping and returns

Ships in seven working days. Returns in fourteen.

Pieces ship across India within seven working days of an order going through. You will get a tracking link the day it leaves the studio.

A return is accepted within fourteen days of delivery if the piece is unworn and the tag is still attached. Because a run is numbered, a returned piece is not folded into general stock. It goes back into the same run, still carrying its own number.

03 / Care

Wash cold. Dry flat.

Wash cold and dry flat. That one line covers most of what a piece needs.

Denim and carpet pieces are not to be tumble dried. The heat is hard on the stitch and on the weight of the cloth, so let them dry the slower way, laid flat, out of direct sun.

04 / Privacy

What we hold, and who holds it.

This site is operated by House of Singully Private Limited, Hyderabad. When you order a piece or write to us, we hold what is needed to make and ship it and to answer you: your name, address, phone number and email. That information is used for that order and that conversation, nothing else, and it is not sold to anyone.

If you want to know what we hold on you, or want it removed, write to hello@singully.com and we will sort it out directly with you.

05 / Terms

The plain version.

This site is operated by House of Singully Private Limited, Hyderabad. Buying a piece here means you have read the sizing, shipping and care information on this page and agree to it. Prices are shown in Indian rupees and hold from the day a run opens to the day it closes.

Questions about an order, a return or anything on this page go to hello@singully.com, and we will answer from there.
